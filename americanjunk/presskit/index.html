// Short Description

Inherit Dad's old machine works in Harmon Falls, a small Rust Belt town. Salvage junk, build and tune machines, fix up his pickup to haul stone and ore from the quarry, and help your neighbors with 20 town projects. A cozy factory game with no combat and no rush.

// Long Description

Burned out at a city office job and stuck in a cramped apartment, you get word that Dad, Frank, has passed away, leaving you his old machine works in Harmon Falls, a small Rust Belt town. You drive out, and Earl, the old caretaker, hands you the key. Now it's up to you to keep the shop going, make useful things out of what other people threw away, and help a small American town get back on its feet.

American Junk is a cozy third-person factory game. There is no combat and no rush.

// Key Features

  • Build your workshop — place, link, power and tune machines; more than 100 recipes, from planks and bolts to mailbox kits, park benches, radios and an old telescope.
  • Salvage and haul — sweep the yard and town edges for daily salvage, fix up Dad's pickup, and haul iron, copper, limestone and clay from Harmon Quarry.
  • Go underground — mine the old timbered tunnel's iron gallery and coal crosscut by survey lamp.
  • Help Harmon Falls — 12 residents and 20 town projects, starting with Bea's water wheel at Mill Creek.
  • Explore and collect — Mill Creek, the Rail Spur, Maple Woods, Foundry Hollow, the quarry and Observatory Ridge; dozens of keepsakes to find.
  • Cozy and fair — no combat, no hunger meter, no real-money shop; goals never expire.

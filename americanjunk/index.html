American Junk
// about the game

Your Dad Died.
You Inherited His Mess.

Somewhere between Detroit and nowhere, there's a factory. Scrap piles, broken machines, a town that grew up around the mess. He ran it. Now it's yours.

Salvage what's left. Set up machines. Sell what you make. Watch shops and streets fill in as the place comes back to life.

That's the loop. That's the whole game.

// common questions

The Basics

What is American Junk?

An iOS factory game. You take over your dad's junkyard plant, salvage scrap, build machines, sell what you make, and grow the town around it.

When does it release?

It's in active development as a TestFlight Prototype. App Store timing isn't announced. Follow Totem Works / Jon for updates.

What platforms?

iOS. TestFlight now, App Store when it's ready. No browser demo, no Steam build, no Windows .exe.

Is there a demo I can play right now?

Public play isn't open yet. Internal TestFlight is how we're proving the day-one path. We'll widen when it's ready.

How much will it cost?

Not locked. We'll post it here when we know.

Who makes it?

Totem Works Studio, LLC — small indie studio, USA, founded 2026.

How can I support?

Follow @TheTotemWorks and @JonLotti, share the game with factory-game friends, and watch for TestFlight / App Store when we open it up.

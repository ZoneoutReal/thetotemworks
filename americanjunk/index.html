// about the game

Your Dad Died.
You Inherited His Mess.

Somewhere between Detroit and nowhere, there's a factory. Broken belts, dead power lines, rust on everything. Your dad ran it. Now it's yours.

Scavenge real Midwest cities — Buffalo, Pittsburgh, Detroit — for materials. Rebuild the grid. Install a Smelter, a Stamping Press, an Assembler. Figure out how they connect. Watch the first belt move. Feel unreasonably proud.

Then craft increasingly absurd American products: hot dog launchers, electric guitars, fireworks, cowboy boots — and eventually, whatever the Bald Eagle Launcher is. That's the dream. That's the whole factory.

// what you'll be doing

Build. Scavenge. Automate. Repeat.

🏭

Factory Building

Lay your own floors, raise walls, install doors. Design a production floor from scratch — every machine placement is a decision.

⚡

Power Grid System

Wire generators to machines with a flood-fill grid. Overload a circuit and risk a fire. Manage load like a responsible adult. You won't.

🔩

Deep Crafting Tree

40+ machines, 100+ recipes, clear tier progression from raw iron to post-modern absurdity. Each unlock feels earned.

📦

Conveyor Logistics

Automate material flow with conveyor belts. Build the pipeline of your dreams. Stare at it for twenty minutes. Add one more belt. Perfect.

🗺️

Rust Belt World

Explore real Midwest cities inspired by Buffalo, Pittsburgh, Detroit. Scavenge abandoned sites. Meet NPCs with very strong opinions about your factory.

🦅

Bald Eagle Launcher

We're not going to explain this. You'll have to get there yourself. It's the end goal. It's what the factory was always for. God bless America.

// the studio

Totem Works, LLC

Totem Works is a small independent game studio making pixel art games that are playful, handcrafted, and built with serious attention to detail.

American Junk is our debut title — a love letter to Midwest factory towns, sandbox games that reward patience, and the beautiful absurdity of making things in America. One scrap at a time.

We're building in public, sharing development updates, and making exactly the game we want to play.

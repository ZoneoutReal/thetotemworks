Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-15

This policy covers two surfaces operated by Totem Works Studio, LLC: the public marketing website at thetotemworks.com, and the free web demo of American Junk hosted on GitHub Pages. They store different things, so each is explained separately below.

// This Website (thetotemworks.com)

The marketing site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure anonymous traffic — which pages get viewed, roughly where visitors are in the world (country-level), which site referred them, and which device they are on. We do not collect names, emails, or any identifier that ties a visit back to an individual person.

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga, _ga_*) and receives anonymized event data. We have IP anonymization enabled where configurable. For Google's side of the story, see their privacy policy.

If you do not want to be counted, either enable your browser's Do-Not-Track / tracking protection, install the official Google Analytics opt-out extension, or block googletagmanager.com in your browser.

// The American Junk Web Demo

The in-game demo has its own zero-PII policy that is independent of Google Analytics. In short, the game only sends anonymous gameplay events ("machine repaired", "session end") tagged with a random session ID that is regenerated each time you open the game. Cloud saves (if you enable them) are keyed on a random 16-character code that lives only in your browser — we never see it unless you paste it to us for support.

We do not collect name, email, address, IP, payment information, browser fingerprint, or ad identifiers from the game.

Full game-side policy, including how to request deletion: american_junk/docs/cloud_save/privacy_policy.md .

// Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or data-access requests: contact Totem Works through the website's Contact link or the email on our GitHub profile. We aim to respond within 30 days.

// Children

The marketing site and demo are not targeted at children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, contact us and we will delete it.

// Changes

We will update the "Last updated" date above whenever this policy changes. Material changes (e.g., adding a new analytics provider, collecting a new category of data) will also be announced in our devlog before they take effect.

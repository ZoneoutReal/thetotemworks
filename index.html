Studio

About Totem Works

Totem Works, LLC is a small independent game studio committed to making games that feel like they were built by someone who actually loves games.

We believe the best games are dense, weird, and rewarding — the kind that give you a system to play with rather than a movie to watch. We're building those games.

Currently in active development on American Junk, a sandbox factory builder set in the American rust belt. Built in Godot 4, designed to run in the browser.
